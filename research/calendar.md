# Episode calendar (030–365): one year of daily episodes

Built from the outlier formulas in `playbook.md` and `report.md`: learn the pattern, never copy a video.
Order mixes categories (a familiar world, a "shouldn't exist" object, a list, a signal or mission, a what-if, a
cosmic-scale question) so no two neighbours feel alike, and the strongest proven topics come first.
Facts must stay true for a year after writing: no "this week", no "just announced"; give years instead.

Format per line: `number | file key | working title | angle`
